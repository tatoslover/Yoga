Improved Flexibility

Yoga poses work by stretching your muscles. They can help you move better and feel less stiff or tired. At any level of yoga, you'll probably start to notice benefits soon. In one study, people improved their flexibility by up to 35% after just 8 weeks of yoga.

Increased Strength

Many yoga poses require you to support your body weight in new ways, including balancing on one leg or supporting yourself with your arms. Holding these poses over several breaths helps build muscular strength and endurance.

Better Posture

When you're stronger and more flexible, your posture improves. Most standing and sitting poses develop core strength, which helps support better posture. With a stronger core, you're more likely to sit and stand tall.

Joint Health

The movements necessary for yoga are low impact, allowing you to use your joints without injuring them. Yoga also helps strengthen the muscles around the joints, lessening their load. People with arthritis often see marked improvement in their pain and mobility with regular gentle yoga practice.

Improved Strength, Balance and Flexibility

Slow movements and deep breathing increase blood flow and warm up muscles, while holding a pose can build strength. Regular practice of yoga is beneficial for people with high blood pressure, heart disease, aches and pains, and more.

Back Pain Relief

Yoga is as good as basic stretching for easing pain and improving mobility in people with lower back pain. After 12 weeks of practicing yoga, people with back pain experience improvements in back function compared to standard medical care.

Heart Health

Regular yoga practice may reduce levels of stress and body-wide inflammation, contributing to healthier hearts. Yoga may address several factors of heart health, including blood pressure and excess weight.

Stress Reduction

Yoga reduces the physical effects of stress on the body. By encouraging relaxation, yoga helps to lower the levels of stress hormones like cortisol. Related benefits include lowering blood pressure and heart rate, improving digestion, and boosting the immune system.

Anxiety Relief

Many people begin practicing yoga as a way to cope with feelings of anxiety. Research suggests that yoga can help reduce anxiety. Yoga emphasizes the importance of being present in the current moment and finding a sense of peace, which can help treat anxiety.

Improved Focus

Regular yoga practice helps train your mind to become more present and focused. Research has found that a consistent yoga practice improves coordination, reaction time, memory, and even IQ scores.

Mood Enhancement

Yoga can help boost your mood. Some studies suggest that yoga may have an anti-depressant effect and could help decrease the symptoms of depression. The combination of meditation, physical movement, and deep breathing increases blood flow to the brain, creating a sense of calm and well-being.

Stress Management

According to the National Institutes of Health, scientific evidence shows that yoga supports stress management, mental health, mindfulness, healthy eating, weight loss and quality sleep.

Mental Well-Being

Regular yoga practice creates mental clarity and calmness, increases body awareness, relieves chronic stress patterns, relaxes the mind, centers attention, and sharpens concentration.

{% endif %} {% if target == "lifestyle" %}

Better Sleep

Research has shown that incorporating yoga into your routine can help promote better sleep. This may be because of yoga's ability to help people deal with stress. Stress is a common cause of sleep problems, including insomnia.

Increased Energy

Contrary to what you might think, certain yoga sequences are actually energizing. Some specific poses, particularly backbends and standing poses, can bring an energy boost to your day. Specific breathing techniques in yoga, called pranayama, can also help increase energy and relieve fatigue.

Better Sleep

Research shows that a consistent bedtime yoga routine can help you get in the right mindset and prepare your body to fall asleep and stay asleep.

More Energy

Yoga focuses on proper alignment, breathing, and bringing awareness to the body's sensations, which can leave you feeling both mentally and physically rejuvenated.

Better Lifestyle Habits

Many practitioners of yoga report that they began making healthier choices in their daily lives after starting yoga. The mindfulness cultivated during yoga practice encourages a heightened awareness of physical and emotional sensations, which may lead to healthier eating and self-care habits.
